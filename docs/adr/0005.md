# ADR-05: Account with a verified email address; card catalog without an account

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-07 (replaces the proposal of a passwordless pseudonymous identity)
- **Revision:** 2026-11-13: the phase 1 server also brings the catalog, decks and history (ADR-13)
- **Reference:** section 10 of the architecture and requirements document; RF-32, RF-33, RF-41, RNF-23 and RNF-24

## Context

Anyone must be able to browse the game's cards without signing up. Every other feature (decks, matches, history and community) is tied to a person and their data, and the GDPR requires keeping only what is necessary, for only as long as necessary.

## Decision

- Without an account, only the card catalog can be used: searching, filtering and viewing each card with its options, plus the screens to sign in and to create an account.
- Everything else requires an account with a valid email address and a password.
- The account stays pending until the confirmation link sent to the email address is opened. If it is not confirmed within 24 hours, the account, the email address and the link are deleted.
- Passwords follow NIST SP 800-63B-4: at least 15 characters, at least 64 allowed, no composition rules or expiry, and checked against a list of common or leaked passwords.
- The server stores passwords with Argon2id (libsodium's `crypto_pwhash_str`) and, for the confirmation link, only a hash.
- Other players only see a pseudonym; the email address is shown to no one.
- Optional second factor with a passkey.

## Rejected alternatives

- A passwordless pseudonymous identity with passkeys (initial proposal): it does not follow the sign-up model with a verified email address.
- Sign-in with Google, Apple or Discord: it hands data over to other companies.
- Requiring an account to see the cards as well: it turns away people who only want to look them up.

## Consequences

- The server sends confirmation and password recovery emails through a provider within the EEA (ADR-07, RNF-12).
- A scheduled task deletes unconfirmed sign-ups as soon as they are 24 hours old; the link stops working after 24 hours even if the task runs late.
- Sign-up, sign-in and recovery give the same answer whether or not the email address exists, with a limit on attempts.
- Local matches also require being signed in, so the account service arrives in phase 1, together with the card catalog and each account's decks and history (ADR-13).
- Android 9 (API 28) stays as the minimum because of Credential Manager passkeys.
