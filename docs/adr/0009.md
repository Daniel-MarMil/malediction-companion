# ADR-09: Develop with the official content and visual identity

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-07
- **Reference:** section 10 of the architecture and requirements document

## Context

The app should feel like the official Malediction experience: card names and images, fonts, colors and icons. The malediction.gg terms forbid reproducing or distributing its content without express permission, even on a non-profit basis.

## Decision

Development uses the official content and visual identity (cards, images, fonts, colors and icons) as if there were a license. Nothing official is published without a written license from Loot Studios.

## Rejected alternatives

- Our own card sheets and a neutral theme from the start: they take the app away from the official experience.
- Publishing the official content without permission.

## Consequences

- The official content lives in a private pack (ADR-12).
- Before any release, free ones included, the license and the brand kit must be requested from Loot Studios.
- The app keeps a neutral theme and its own card sheets (RF-03) for when the official pack is missing.
