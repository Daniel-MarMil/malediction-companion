# ADR-07: Hosting and providers only in the EEA

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** 2026-11-07
- **Reference:** section 10 of the architecture and requirements document

## Context

The data controller is in the EU, and the app must comply with the GDPR without international transfers.

## Decision

Servers, backups and email providers within the EEA; no push notifications in the MVP.

## Rejected alternatives

- Clouds with transfers outside the EEA.

## Consequences

- No push notifications in the MVP: FCM and APNs are Google and Apple services and would need a separate assessment.
- A hosting provider in the EEA must be chosen before the online match phase.
